# BUG-006 — Press Center Page Content Has No Left Padding

**Bug ID:** BUG-006  
**Title:** Press Center page content is aligned too close to the left edge of the browser window  
**Module:** Footer / Press Center Page / UI  
**Severity:** Low  
**Priority:** Medium  

**Environment:**
- OS: Windows 11
- Browser: Google Chrome
- Browser Mode: Incognito
- Website: https://comfy.ua/ua/

### Preconditions
1. Open the COMFY website.
2. Scroll down to the footer section.

### Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the COMFY homepage: https://comfy.ua/ua/
2. Scroll down to the footer.
3. Locate the "Прес-центр" link in the "Comfy" section.
4. Click the link.
5. Wait until the Press Center page loads.
6. Observe the alignment of the page heading and text content.

### Expected Result
The page heading and text content should have appropriate left padding or margins. The content should be properly aligned and visually consistent with the website layout.

### Actual Result
The page heading and text content are displayed very close to the left edge of the browser window, without sufficient left padding or margin. This makes the page look poorly aligned and reduces visual readability.

### Severity
Low — The issue does not prevent users from accessing or reading the information, but it negatively affects the visual presentation.

### Priority
Medium — The layout should be adjusted to provide a consistent and user-friendly interface.
